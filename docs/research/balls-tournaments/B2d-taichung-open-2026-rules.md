3. COMPETITION RULES & FORMAT

a. Minor League Pickleball (MiLP) Team Challenge Format

• Governed by the 2026 Official Tournament Rulebook of USA Pickleball (USAP).
• Player Entry Limit: Each competitor is restricted to entering a maximum of one (1) MiLP team division.
• 1) Match Rules: Phase 1 - Round Robin Stage:

    • Scoring System: 21-point rally scoring format; teams must win by a 2-point margin. Each team is allowed one
         (1) standard timeout per game.

    • Match Sequence: Each team match consists of 4 games played in order: Women's Doubles, Men's Doubles,
         Mixed Doubles #1, Mixed Doubles #2. All 4 games must be completed.

    • Tie-Breaker (Dreambreaker): If games tie at 2:2, a Dreambreaker will immediately be played (singles rotation,
         4 rallies per cycle, played to 21 points, rally scoring, win by 2).

    • Pre-Match Coin Toss / Number Selection: Select 1/2 or coin flip to choose: (a) Home or Away team, (b) Serve
         or Receive across games, (c) Starting court side for Women's Doubles.

    • Positioning & Serving: Players stay on designated sides (Right side serves on even scores; Left side serves on
         odd scores). When leading team reaches 11 points, teams may switch court sides.

    • Scoring Shift: When any team reaches 20 points, scoring automatically switches to traditional side-out scoring.
• 2) Group Standings: Standings Tie-Breaker: Total Wins → Head-to-Head → Net Points [(Total Points Won) −

     (Total Points Lost)] → Lucky Draw by Head Referee.

b. Individual Competition Format

• Governed by the 2026 Official Tournament Rulebook of USA Pickleball (USAP).
• Player Entry Limit: Each competitor is restricted to entering a maximum of one (1) event per day.
• Scoring Rules: Singles Matches: 1 game to 11 points; switch sides at 6 points. No DEUCE (first to 11 points wins).
• Doubles Rules: Doubles Matches: 1 game to 15 points; switch sides at 8 points. No DEUCE (first to 15 points

     wins).
• Skill Ratings: Player Skill Level: Ratings shall comply with the Chinese Taipei Pickleball Association (CTPA)

     grading system. The organizing committee reserves the right to determine/adjust player skill levels.
• Refeering: Disputes: Any controversy regarding player qualifications, rules interpretation, or referee calls shall

     be resolved by the Head Referee, whose decision is final.
• Round Robin Standings: Tie-Breaker Sequence: Total Match Wins → Head-to-Head Result → Net Points [(Total

     Points Won) − (Total Points Lost)] → Lucky Draw by Head Referee.

壹拾、 競賽規則：
        MILP 團體賽賽制:

      一、比賽規則採用美國匹克球協會審定公布之2026年版匹克球官方錦標賽規則。
      二、每人以報名1組為限

三、第一階段為小組循環賽
      (1)採21分回合得分制,淨勝2分獲獲勝(有1次標準暫停)。
      (2)每場比賽包含四局賽事,順序是女雙,男雙,混雙,混雙;四點皆打。
      當勝局數為2:2 時,則進行Dreambreak加賽決定勝負。
      (Dreambreaker:比賽將由每個隊伍重新排列單打成員,按先後順序上場,每四分
      交換一組選手,直到有隊伍累計達到21分並以兩分優勢獲勝。)
      (3)選手通過選擇數字1或者2,或者硬幣來決定以下三個選項中做出選擇：
          a.選擇成為主隊或客隊
          b.選擇每局比賽的發球方或接發球方
          c.選擇女子雙打比賽的起始場地。
      (4) 球員換邊/發球：
      在每回合得分制中，球員得分後不會交換場地。 A球員在右側發球，當比分是
      偶數時由A球員發球；B球員在左側發球，當比分是奇數時由B球員發球。 A球員
      和B球員也必須在各自的場地接發球。當先攻方達到11分時，可以在換邊或暫停
      期間選擇交換球員場地。雙方必須在比賽恢復前宣布換邊。
      (5)當每局比賽中的任何隊伍獲得20分的時候,比賽由回合得分制轉為發球得分
      制。
      (6)循環賽名次排序
      a.勝場數
      b.遇兩隊勝場數相同時，視雙方比賽勝者為勝。
      c.若遇三隊或三隊以上勝場數相同時，依下列先後順序判定：
      1. (總勝分) －(總負分)之得失分大者為勝。
      2. 如仍無法判定名次時，則由裁判長主持抽籤決定。
